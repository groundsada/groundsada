# Mohammad Firas Sada

**Research Networking Systems Software Engineer | HPC & AI/ML | FPGA | Cloud & DevOps**

I am a research networking systems software engineer at ESnet, the Energy Sciences Network, owned by the U.S. Department of Energy and operated by Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. My work sits at science data movement and programmable networks: SENSE orchestration, transfer tooling for high-energy physics, and the infrastructure between the tools researchers actually use. I work remotely.

Previously at the San Diego Supercomputer Center (UC San Diego), I worked on the National Research Platform Kubernetes substrate, FABRIC integration, segment routing on FPGAs, CUDA tooling, Prometheus exporters for novel accelerators, and P4 machine learning on SmartNICs.
